Or do the whole deployment yourself

Six transactions, signed by you. Nothing leaves your wallet except gas.

  1. Deploy a 6-decimal test USDC token
  2. Deploy the escrow, naming the API’s arbiter key
  3. Mint 1,000,000 USDC to the buyer account — the API signs as that account, not as you
  4. Send 0.003 ETH to the buyer account for gas
  5. Send 0.003 ETH to the seller account for gas
  6. Send 0.003 ETH to the arbiter for gas

All three signer accounts need gas: buyer funds and confirms, seller ships, arbiter refunds. The USDC goes to the buyer account for the same reason — the API signs as that account, not as you.
